# Cypher: first contact

Wall Tones story draft v0.1 · 11 November 2026

**For Oded's review. No dialogue, audio, visual effect or progression rule is implemented by this document.**

## What comes from your brief

- A dialogue-heavy story breaks into the game with static and radio squawks.
- First contact comes after a few rounds following a five-pattern streak. Cypher then asks for ten and increasingly longer streaks to open longer communication windows.
- Cypher is a spy hacking a terrorist organization's computerized defenses. Your machine has an unusual quantum connection to the target. The modifiers are part of Cypher's intervention in the game's fiction.
- Something goes wrong for the spy, then gets resolved.
- Luminous cracks appear on the desktop page or on walls in mixed reality (MR), then disappear when the player finishes a pattern.
- Possible MR encounter: a drone appears above the player after they have been looking down; Cypher asks for a streak to disable it; it falls apart on the floor.
- Cypher's voice is the opposite gender to the selected game narrator, using a separate performance distinct from either existing narrator voice. Both casting combinations need their own Cypher voice, not a swap to the other narrator recording.

Everything below that adds exact timing, new lines, plot turns, names, rewards or behavior is an **editorial proposal**, not an approved feature. The opening has been edited for spoken delivery; your supplied wording is retained in the final appendix.

## Story shape

**Proposed episode title:** First contact.

Cypher first treats the player as a useful anonymous signal. The narrator treats Cypher as an unauthorized interruption. They gradually stop arguing and work together. The supposed hacking link turns out to be a bridge between rooms in different dimensions. Cypher knew part of this and hid it. The breach lets an enemy drone cross; the player closes its return channel and saves Cypher. The ending resolves this immediate mission while leaving a quieter second mystery.

The quantum explanation is deliberately science fiction spoken by a character. It is not a real account of quantum computing, entanglement or cryptographic key recovery. Later dialogue revises Cypher's first explanation instead of presenting it as technical instruction.

**Performance:** Cypher begins clipped, careful and close to the microphone. Fear leaks through before the admission. Warmth is earned near the end. The narrator stays dry and precise, not villainous. The player answers through play in this draft; no microphone or spoken response is required. Optional yes/no voice or nod/headshake input is under feasibility review and has not been selected or implemented. Any later version needs explicit prompts, a button fallback and device checks; this script does not assume Quest speech recognition support.

## Proposed communication grammar

All numbers here are tuning proposals except the requested five and ten anchors.

1. A window is a story beat, not a line after every pattern. Deliver one short exchange at a safe boundary after player input and before the next listen phase. Never talk over the musical pattern.
2. Opening proposal: first reach streak 5, then complete three further patterns. Give the first three short contacts at those boundaries; the third asks for streak 10. Confirm whether you meant three extra patterns, three rounds/runs, or another delay.
3. At streak 10, allow a longer contact split into short chunks at successive safe boundaries. Do not play a long monologue all at once. Queue one story beat at a time, even if a player passes several thresholds quickly.
4. Later provisional gates: 12, 15, 18, 20, 22, 25, 28 and 30. These are not difficulty commitments. A stage may contain several exchanges at the same gate before asking for the next one.
5. Proposed failure handling: no lost story progress and no punishment for missing a note. Retry the gameplay normally; resume the uncompleted story beat once its condition is met. Do not repeat already completed scenes each run. The exact relationship to streak savers remains open.
6. Proposed save rule: save the last completed scene locally, independently from best streak and currency. Reloading must not duplicate a reward or strand a drone. Resetting the story should be a separate explicit action, not an incidental stats reset.
7. Proposed accessibility: captions identify CYPHER and NARRATOR, including meaningful sound cues. Voice-off retains dialogue text; reduced-effects mode removes flashes, jump scares and falling debris. No surprise loud static. Story opt-in, pause/skip/replay controls and narration priority need approval before implementation.
8. Requests such as “get to fifteen” refer to the run streak only if that definition is approved. We must settle whether reaching a new gate on a later run counts, whether gates are cumulative, and whether story mode can adapt for players who cannot reach thirty.

## Sound direction

- **RADIO OPEN:** a short squelch, a thin band of static, then a slightly unstable voice. Keep the musical pads clear and louder than incidental hiss.
- **RADIO CLOSE:** clipped carrier chirp, not an explosive cut.
- **SIGNAL FRACTURE:** fragments of the same voice delayed, never a new instruction hidden under a pattern.
- **CRACK:** a quiet glass-like resonance and light seam, not a real-wall damage claim.
- **DRONE:** distant mechanical flutter, becoming directional in MR. No sudden maximum-volume sound above the head.
- Exact levels, timings and filters are proposed sound direction. No audio has been generated and no paid voice service is required by this draft.

## Script

Each block is a proposed scene. Stage cues are not spoken. Line IDs stay stable for recording, subtitles and revision notes. Quoted opening revisions are editorial versions of your supplied lines; the rest is new writing.

### 01 · First carrier

**Proposed gate:** streak 5, then first additional completed pattern. Opening rewrite.

[Low static. Two failed carrier clicks. A voice cuts in very briefly.]

**C01 · CYPHER:** “Connection... established.”

**N01 · NARRATOR:** “That channel is not part of the game.”

[Carrier drops. Leave the next pattern clean.]

### 02 · Someone is listening

**Proposed gate:** next completed pattern after scene 01. Opening rewrite plus new narrator line.

[Short radio squawk. Cypher sounds relieved but keeps quiet.]

**C02 · CYPHER:** “Can you hear me? You're doing great. I'll explain in the next communication window.”

**N02 · NARRATOR:** “I can hear you. That is the problem.”

**C03 · CYPHER:** “Not you. The person playing.”

### 03 · Ten opens the channel

**Proposed gate:** next completed pattern after scene 02. Opening rewrite.

**C04 · CYPHER:** “Yes. Keep playing. They don't suspect anything. Good.”

**N03 · NARRATOR:** “Who are ‘they’?”

**C05 · CYPHER:** “Get a streak of ten or more. Then I can send a longer message.”

**N04 · NARRATOR:** “An unusually demanding way to introduce yourself.”

[Radio closes. No further story until the next gate.]

### 04 · Cypher

**Proposed gate:** streak 10. Longer window, divided into three short exchanges at safe pattern boundaries. Editorial rewrite of the supplied exposition.

[The radio is clearer. A faint room tone behind Cypher: ventilation and a distant metal knock.]

**C06 · CYPHER:** “My codename is Cypher. I'm inside a terrorist organization's defense network.”

**N05 · NARRATOR:** “Inside the network, or inside the building?”

**C07 · CYPHER:** “Both. I'd prefer to leave both.”

[Next safe boundary.]

**C08 · CYPHER:** “Their quantum server keeps changing the lock. I need the hash key before the next reset.”

**N06 · NARRATOR:** “And you expect to find it in a memory game?”

**C09 · CYPHER:** “I found a machine carrying the same entanglement signature as their server. Yours.”

[Next safe boundary.]

**C10 · CYPHER:** “Keep getting those sequences right. I've changed parts of your game to help. The modifiers? That was me.”

**N07 · NARRATOR:** “You changed my game?”

**C11 · CYPHER:** “I improved your chances.”

**N08 · NARRATOR:** “Those are different statements.”

**C12 · CYPHER:** “Reach twelve. I can hold the next window a little longer.”

### 05 · The first seam

**Proposed gate:** streak 12. First visual breach is a proposal drawn from your crack idea.

[A narrow luminous crack appears away from the pads and captions. In MR it follows a suitable wall surface. It does not look like real structural damage.]

**C13 · CYPHER:** “I'm through the outer lock. Tell me the room hasn't changed.”

**N09 · NARRATOR:** “There is light where there should not be light.”

**C14 · CYPHER:** “All right. Don't touch it. Finish the next pattern.”

[On successful pattern completion, the crack closes. If the player fails, do not leave an escalating effect running indefinitely. Exact fallback is an open design decision.]

**N10 · NARRATOR:** “It closed.”

**C15 · CYPHER:** “Good. You can close them from your side.”

**N11 · NARRATOR:** “You sound as though you just learned that.”

**C16 · CYPHER:** “Fifteen. Please.”

### 06 · The name behind the voice

**Proposed gate:** streak 15. Emotional beat, not another explanation dump.

**C17 · CYPHER:** “There's a door here with no handle on this side.”

**N12 · NARRATOR:** “You said you were hacking a defense system.”

**C18 · CYPHER:** “I am. My partner got through the door. Then the lock reset.”

**N13 · NARRATOR:** “You could have started with that.”

**C19 · CYPHER:** “Would you have believed me?”

**N14 · NARRATOR:** “I would have asked fewer questions about the game.”

[Something taps three times beyond Cypher's door. Cypher taps twice in reply.]

**C20 · CYPHER:** “They're still there.”

**N15 · NARRATOR:** “Then we keep the channel open.”

**C21 · CYPHER:** “Eighteen. The next lock needs a longer sequence.”

### 07 · The truth in the static

**Proposed gate:** streak 18. The science-fiction premise becomes the twist, not a factual claim.

[Two small seams briefly align. A radio echo answers Cypher a fraction late.]

**N16 · NARRATOR:** “Those are not errors in the display. They move when the room moves.”

**C22 · CYPHER:** “The machine isn't only a computer. It holds a bridge between places.”

**N17 · NARRATOR:** “You said we were finding a key.”

**C23 · CYPHER:** “That was the short version.”

**N18 · NARRATOR:** “It was the dishonest version.”

**C24 · CYPHER:** “I knew it could open a door here. I didn't know it could open one there.”

[Let the silence land.]

**C25 · CYPHER:** “I'm sorry. You didn't agree to that.”

**N19 · NARRATOR:** “Then no more surprises.”

**C26 · CYPHER:** “No more secrets. Twenty gets me to the control room. Then we shut the bridge down.”

### 08 · The interrupted warning

**Proposed gate:** streak 20. Cypher is discovered; no real-world danger claim.

[An alarm is heard only through the radio. Cypher whispers.]

**C27 · CYPHER:** “Control room. I can see the bridge controls.”

**N20 · NARRATOR:** “And your partner?”

**C28 · CYPHER:** “On the other side of the glass. Alive.”

[A metal impact. Carrier distorts.]

**C29 · CYPHER:** “They've found my signal. If you see something come through, don't follow it. Keep your feet where they are. I can...”

[Transmission cuts. No additional pattern plays until the scene boundary ends.]

**N21 · NARRATOR:** “Cypher?”

[One quiet unanswered carrier chirp.]

**N22 · NARRATOR:** “We still have the pattern. Let's finish it.”

### 09 · The drone

**Proposed gate:** streak 22 plus a separate, approved encounter cue. MR drone is optional, not a committed feature. Flat fallback may use an on-page silhouette or remain audio/captions only.

[A mechanical flutter above and ahead, never requiring the player to walk. Optional MR staging: after a period of downward attention, reveal it at a safe visible distance. Exact gaze trigger and surface placement need approval and device testing.]

**N23 · NARRATOR:** “Something crossed the seam.”

**C30 · CYPHER:** “I'm here. That's a scout. It followed the open channel.”

**N24 · NARRATOR:** “Can it hear us?”

**C31 · CYPHER:** “Only me. Keep playing. Reach twenty-five and I can break its link.”

**N25 · NARRATOR:** “No chasing it. No ducking under furniture. The pads are all you need.”

**C32 · CYPHER:** “That's right. Let it watch. Give me the sequence.”

### 10 · Cut the link

**Proposed gate:** streak 25. Resolve the drone encounter without a new physical task.

[The drone holds still. Its light follows the same pulse as the crack, not a blinding flash.]

**C33 · CYPHER:** “There's the link. One complete pattern. Then I can cut it.”

**N26 · NARRATOR:** “Listen first. We'll wait.”

[After a successful pattern, its light goes out. If MR debris is approved, parts settle onto a validated floor as a non-colliding visual and fade. No reaching down or picking up required.]

**C34 · CYPHER:** “Disconnected.”

**N27 · NARRATOR:** “A remarkably poor score for a flying computer.”

**C35 · CYPHER:** “You made a joke.”

**N28 · NARRATOR:** “You made a mess.”

**C36 · CYPHER:** “Fair. Twenty-eight. We close the doors next.”

### 11 · Two doors

**Proposed gate:** streak 28. Cypher must save their partner without abandoning the player's room.

**C37 · CYPHER:** “The bridge has two controls. One here. One beyond the glass.”

**N29 · NARRATOR:** “Your partner can reach the other one?”

[Three taps. Two taps in reply.]

**C38 · CYPHER:** “Yes. But when we close it, I lose this channel.”

**N30 · NARRATOR:** “You were always supposed to leave.”

**C39 · CYPHER:** “I know. I just wanted to say thank you before...”

[A brief, gentle squelch obscures the end, not an extended tease.]

**N31 · NARRATOR:** “Say it after. Thirty first.”

**C40 · CYPHER:** “Thirty. Then one last window.”

### 12 · One last window

**Proposed gate:** streak 30. Finale: no requirement to beat a secretly higher target.

[The radio clears completely for the first time. Cracks hold a soft steady light.]

**C41 · CYPHER:** “We're both at the controls.”

**N32 · NARRATOR:** “And the defense network?”

**C42 · CYPHER:** “Isolated. No more doors. They can't send anything else through.”

**N33 · NARRATOR:** “Then let's close ours.”

**C43 · CYPHER:** “Finish this pattern. No rush. I'll stay on the line.”

[Next successful pattern closes the seams. Pause or failure must not destroy the ending; recovery rules need approval.]

**C44 · CYPHER:** “It's closing. We're out.”

**N34 · NARRATOR:** “Both of you?”

**C45 · CYPHER:** “Both. You didn't just open a door. You brought us home.”

**N35 · NARRATOR:** “We mostly pressed four buttons.”

**C46 · CYPHER:** “You kept going.”

[One clean carrier chirp. The radio fades, not a scream or crash.]

### 13 · Back to the game

**Proposed gate:** first calm boundary after the ending. Epilogue and optional sequel hook.

**N36 · NARRATOR:** “Connection closed. Room intact. Mostly.”

[Brief pause.]

**N37 · NARRATOR:** “Ready for another pattern?”

[Optional later tag, not immediately over the next pattern: three soft taps, answered by two carrier clicks.]

**N38 · NARRATOR:** “That had better be a thank-you.”

[End of episode. Free play continues. No real hacking or communication with a third party occurs.]

## Recovery lines: proposal, not failure spam

Use only if needed, with a cooldown and no interruption of pad audio. Ordinary missed patterns do not need story chatter.

- **R01 · CYPHER, after a failed critical pattern:** “Still here. We can try again.”
- **R02 · NARRATOR, after a failed critical pattern:** “Nothing lost. Listen when you're ready.”
- **R03 · CYPHER, on a resumed unfinished scene:** “Same channel. Same plan. Take your time.”
- **R04 · NARRATOR, if a window must wait:** “Message held. The pattern comes first.”
- **R05 · CYPHER, optional gentler gate prompt:** “No deadline on your side. I'll wait for the next clear window.”

## Decisions before gameplay work

1. **Unlock and choice:** automatic story after streak five, an explicit story-mode toggle, or a store unlock? Existing backlog items 14 (story) and 31 (story voice store item) overlap but do not settle this.
2. **Streak definition and delay:** consecutive completed patterns in one run? Does the opening wait three more patterns or several rounds? Do later windows use a new run or an all-time milestone?
3. **Difficulty:** keep the proposed 12 to 30 ladder, use five-step gates, or let communications accumulate across runs? A thirty-pattern requirement could hide the ending from many players.
4. **Modifiers and economy:** is Cypher merely explaining existing modifiers in fiction, or should story award new abilities? This draft awards none and spends no Bars. Do not claim credit for mechanics before a player has actually seen them.
5. **Cast and dialogue:** approve the narrator talking back, the partner, Cypher's partial deception, the dimension twist and the dry humor. No player speech input in this version.
6. **Effects and comfort:** approve cracks first; treat the drone and debris as a later optional slice. Decide MR gaze staging, flat presentation, visibility, reduced effects and what happens when floor or wall mapping is unavailable.
7. **Persistence and repeats:** approve checkpoint saving, replay controls, skip behavior, reset behavior and exact interaction with game over and streak savers.
8. **Voice and sound:** approve final words and casting before generating clips. Set audio mix/ducking rules so a radio cue never changes the memory task by masking notes.
